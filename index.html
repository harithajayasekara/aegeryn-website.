AEGIS Rescue is an early-stage concept for human-supervised aerial search. The goal: systematic coverage, possible-person detection, clear operator reporting, and eventually resilient coordination between multiple autonomous scouts.

We begin in simulation. Capabilities shown here are design intentions, not operational claims.

03 / Engineering principlesDESIGNED FOR TRUST
01 —

Human authority.

People remain responsible for decisions and operational outcomes. Autonomous systems assist; they do not independently authorize rescue dispatch or use of force.

02 —

Resilience first.

Design for failures, degraded communications, transparent system state, and measurable recovery—not just ideal conditions.

03 —

Evidence over hype.

Prove performance through reproducible simulation and controlled testing before making field-readiness claims.
